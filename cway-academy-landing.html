Who We Are

About Cway Academy

A Bible-based leadership training project of CWAY MISSIONS, Bangalore, India. God laid a great vision in our hearts to equip members and pastors of rural churches in India as frontline leaders.

The Challenge We Face

Bridging the Gap in Leadership Training

"Thousands of untrained pastors and lay leaders in remote villages in India have never had the opportunity for formal leadership training or theological education, leaving them vulnerable to false teachings."

India is home to many ethnic groups and languages, making Christian leadership training the most challenging task for churches and theological institutions.

Family Dependency

Most pastors in villages have families dependent on their work, hindering them from going anywhere for extended study.

Financial Barriers

The financial status of most churches cannot support theological training. It remains a dream for most.

The Language Divide

Formal theological training is in English, but most speak only their local languages.

Our Response

Responding to the Crisis

We envision responding to the crisis of insufficiently trained Christian leadership in remote villages in India by providing biblically based leadership training to pastors and lay leaders in their places of ministry.

👁️

Vision

To equip Christ's disciples as frontline leaders through theological education, leadership training, and ministerial practice to fulfil Christ's Great Commission.

🎯

Mission

Holistic, hands-on training for indigenous pastors, leaders, and believers in local churches through a globally certified hybrid learning experience.
